# Pilot Sociolinguistic Survey Results

This directory contains the results of the first Sens-Ex sociolinguistic perception survey, conducted in 2025.

The pilot survey results are available in Excel format: [`sociolinguistic_pilot_survey_results.xlsx`](sociolinguistic_pilot_survey_results.xlsx).

For direct consultation on GitHub, browse the [CSV exports](csv/README.md).


## Survey Protocol

Ten audiovisual excerpts from the Sens-Ex corpus were presented to participants as stimuli.

The survey questionnaire addressed two dimensions:

1. **Emotional responses** elicited by the audiovisual excerpts.
2. **Perceptions of discursive excess**, assessed across different communicative dimensions.

Two successive versions of the questionnaire were developed:

- **Version 0.0:** Initial pilot questionnaire used to evaluate and refine the survey protocol.
- **Version 1.0:** Revised online questionnaire used for the perception study.

The questionnaire is documented in Appendix A (pp. 8–9) of *Building CMC Datasets in the Post-Digital Era: Corpus-Linguistic Principles for Participatory Corpus Construction*.

## Survey Questionnaire

### Question 1 — Emotional Response

**Which emotion do you experience while watching this video?**

Possible responses:

- None
- Fear
- Anger
- Joy
- Surprise
- Sadness
- Disgust
- Boredom

The corresponding results are recorded in the Excel workbook under columns prefixed with `emo_`.

### Question 2 — Perception of Discursive Excess

**Do you consider this video excessive?**

Participants assessed the following communicative dimensions using a three-point scale:

| Score | Response       |
|-------|----------------|
| 0     | Not excessive  |
| 1     | Excessive      |
| 2     | Very excessive |

The dimensions assessed were:

1. The video as a whole.
2. The topic.
3. The speakers' voice (e.g., too low, too high, too loud, too quiet).
4. The speakers' gestures.
5. The speakers' facial expressions.
6. The tone of voice (e.g., too angry, too monotonous, too neutral).
7. The speaking rate (e.g., too fast, too slow, too many or too few pauses).
8. The lexical choices (e.g., too informal, too formal, too vulgar, too many fixed expressions).
9. The discourse as a whole (e.g., too repetitive, unclear, difficult to follow).
10. Turn-taking (e.g., speakers interrupt each other too often).
11. Other (please specify).

The corresponding results are recorded under columns prefixed with `exc_`.

## Interpretation of Survey Results

The Excel workbook contains aggregated results for the ten audiovisual excerpts presented in the survey.

The `emo_*` columns correspond to participants' reported emotional responses, while the `exc_*` columns correspond to their assessments of discursive excess across the communicative dimensions listed above.

The public workbook and CSV exports omit student contributor names, institutional sharing fields, and historical downloaded filenames that can contain student names.
