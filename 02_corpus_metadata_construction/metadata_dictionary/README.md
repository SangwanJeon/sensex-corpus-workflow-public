# Metadata Dictionary

## Overview

This directory contains the metadata dictionary developed for the Sens-Ex corpus:

[`sensex_metadata_set.pdf`](sensex_metadata_set.pdf)

It documents the metadata fields used to describe the collected audiovisual samples, their production contexts, preliminary assessments of discursive excess, and semiotic and communicative characteristics.

The documented metadata can be grouped into three main categories.

## 1. Production and Platform-Related Metadata

These metadata describe the original audiovisual materials, their sources, and their publication contexts.

They include information such as:

- Original video title.
- Content creator or channel name.
- Source URL and platform.
- Publication and collection dates.
- Duration of the original audiovisual material.
- Start and end times of the selected excerpt.
- Platform-specific engagement metrics, where available (views, likes, shares, subscribers, etc.).

These metadata were manually verified against the available source information by student interns and the technical coordinator.

**Note:** several source URLs may be no longer accessible because the original content has been deleted or made unavailable by its creator, or because the platform’s retention period has expired. For example, Twitch generally retains past broadcasts for a limited period, which varies according to the broadcaster’s account status. Consequently, some source materials may no longer be available for independent verification.

## 2. Preliminary Judgments of Discursive Excess

This category documents preliminary assessments concerning the presence or absence of discursive excess in the selected materials.

These assessments are distinguished from the results of the sociolinguistic perception surveys conducted within the Sens-Ex research framework.

The results of the first sociolinguistic perception survey, conducted in 2025, are available in the [pilot survey workbook](../pilot_survey/sociolinguistic_pilot_survey_results.xlsx) and the [pilot survey columns CSV](../pilot_survey/csv/PilotSurveyColumns.csv).

## 3. Semiotic and Communicative Metadata

These metadata describe the semiotic modalities and communicative characteristics of the selected excerpts.

They include information concerning:

- The presence and characteristics of oral and written communication.
- The communicative situation and interactional context (e.g., livestream interactions, live chats, singing, and background music) 
- The presence of code-switching or code-mixing involving different languages or language varieties (including dialects).

These metadata were documented under the supervision of the Sens-Ex coordinating research team.

## Limitations

The metadata dictionary reflects the methodological conventions adopted during Sens-Ex corpus construction.

Some field definitions and controlled vocabularies remain provisional and may differ from the values recorded in the metadata workbooks.
